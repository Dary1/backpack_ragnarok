# 1. game Golden — v2 (ratified 2026-07-02)

Craft your own magical backpacks — not just what fills them, but their very shapes —
and tile them together across your canvas. Slot items like puzzle pieces, then bind
pack to pack through your Units' connection rays, weaving a synergy circuit that is yours alone:
nothing but your arrangement decides your strength, and no guide can copy it.

Every pack speaks through a single Unit. A giant pack carries an arsenal but speaks
with one voice; a swarm of small packs chatters in chains. Find the layout where
everything fits and links perfectly — then watch the exact moment it ignites and
annihilates your enemies. March a Troop of four Squads at a time, alone or with friends, conquer
dungeons for new pieces, shapes, and relics, gamble Transmutators to reroll fate, and
send your caravan marching even while you sleep. Let the crafting of never-before-seen
combos begin.

## 2. Canvas Design Pillars
- **P1: Fit is power.** Spatial arrangement is the primary skill.
- **P2: The Moment is sacred.** All tension lives in arrangement; combat pays it off legibly.
- **P3: Discovery is content.** The synergy space stays deep and partially hidden.
- **P4: Co-op changes builds, not just numbers.**
- **P5: The Unreplicable Self.** (promoted from review's "golden indicator") No two players' canvases should converge on identical builds.
  - A Unit is the vessel; strength always derives from the pack's contents and
    arrangement, never from the Unit alone.

## 3. Core Loop [GREEN, simplified]
- **Run-based roguelite — confirmed.**
- Dungeons: **simple repeated auto-battles managed by schedules**
- Death: run loot lost, **meta progression kept — confirmed**.
- **Frozen Canvas [DECIDED, review round 3]:** a sortie locks the canvas as-is until
  the dungeon is fully traversed. All building happens pre-sortie on the Edit Screen;
  rewards are granted on completion. This is what enables auto-battle and offline
  scheduled runs. **No Gold exists** — Transmutators are the only currency.
- Codex / collection meta. Strength comes from the canvas only; no external power
  or knowledge meta systems.

## 4. Combat
Dugeon Deployments are all scheduled.
No such thing as "join a party, go to dungeon and disband".
A player joins to a schedule. when the time comes, it starts.
- Auto-resolving Clash; meaningful choice front-loaded into arrangement [GREEN].
- Telegraphed charge-ups; **Ragnarok Frame** slow-mo on lethal ignition [GREEN].
- **Asymmetric combat [DECIDED, review round 2]:** enemies do **NOT** have backpack
  systems. Only players hold canvases; enemies run bespoke stat/behavior algorithms.
