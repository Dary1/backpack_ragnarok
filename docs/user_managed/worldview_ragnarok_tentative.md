# Worldview & Ragnarok Endgame — TENTATIVE (not goldenized)

## The Pledge
"Prepare yourself for the coming Ragnarok.
If, through Devotion, you ascend to Einherjar, you are promised the glory of standing
shoulder to shoulder with Odin at Ragnarok, to face the battle beyond."

## Mechanics (as specified)
- **Devotion:** a devoted character **loses everything** (full loss) and is registered as an **Einherjar**.
- Einherjar **auto-participate in the season-end Ragnarok** battle.
- Their performance ("activity level") at Ragnarok is **permanently displayed on a ranking**.
- This is **separate from the weekly boss**.
- Note: death in the main loop still keeps meta progression; only Devotion is full-loss.

## Monetization concept tied to Ragnarok
- Single monetization axis: **"Pay to Kill (glorify of death) players."**
- Buyers purchase **enemy monsters that appear at Ragnarok** and receive a detailed
  report of what their monster did (who it killed, etc.).
- Primary buyer motivation: influencers bragging on social media.
- Deeply coupled to Ragnarok design. **no monetization but only from the pay to kill.**.
